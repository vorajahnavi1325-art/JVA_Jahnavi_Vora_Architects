COMMON GROUND — COMMUNITY CENTRE · LEN+K PRAGUE

Shipping containers,
shared stories.

Designed for 20–25 people, this low-cost community centre brings colourful containers together beneath two alternative roofs—canvas or profiled metal.

A small space for everyday connection.

02 — SITE

A small footprint
with a shared centre.

The project gathers a compact programme around an open communal space, allowing the architecture to work as a place for meeting, eating, pausing and everyday exchange.

03 — PLAN / ORGANISATION
04 — TWO ROOF STRATEGIES

One community.
Two ways to cover it.

Two lightweight roof alternatives create different atmospheres while keeping the same compact container-based programme.

05 — MATERIAL LANGUAGE

Colour becomes
wayfinding.

Mustard yellow, deep blue, timber, fabric and profiled metal give the small centre a clear identity while keeping the construction direct and economical.

06 — DAYLIGHT / ENTRY
09 — EVERYDAY CONNECTION

A place to
stay awhile.

The open plan allows small groups to occupy the centre in different ways, from informal conversations to shared meals and quiet work.
