BETWEEN INSIDE & OUT — BALCONY RENOVATION · FREELANCE

A small edge,
made livable.

A compact renovation that turns an overlooked edge of the home into a warm, intimate retreat. The design brings together built-in seating, layered lighting, timber-toned surfaces and planting.

The result sits between an interior room and an open balcony: enclosed enough to feel comfortable, open enough to remain connected to the outside.

02 — CONCEPT

Designing
the threshold.

The first studies explore how a narrow edge can feel like a complete room. Curved furniture softens the geometry, while vertical textures and shelving build a quieter backdrop around the seating area.

03 — FORM / COMPOSITION
04 — SPATIAL LANGUAGE

Softness meets
structure.

The space balances a strong architectural frame with softer domestic elements. Vertical wall texture, warm timber, curved seating and small pockets of greenery create a layered threshold between inside and out.

05 — MATERIAL & LIGHT

Warm layers,
quiet detail.

A restrained palette keeps the compact space calm. Timber tones bring warmth, ribbed surfaces add depth, and soft pendant lighting creates a comfortable evening atmosphere without overwhelming the room.

06 — ROOM / THRESHOLD
09 — EVERYDAY RETREAT

A place to
pause.

The renovation is intentionally compact: every surface and object contributes to the feeling of a small room, while the opening keeps the space visually connected to the larger home and the outdoors.

10 — INTERIOR / DETAIL
11 — THE FINISHED SPACE

From leftover edge
to everyday room.

The final composition brings together the architectural shell, built-in elements, furniture and light into one small, coherent retreat.
