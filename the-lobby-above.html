THE LOBBY ABOVE

A business hotel shaped around arrival, gathering and light.

THE LOBBY ABOVE explores a hospitality environment where work, rest and social interaction overlap. The project brings together a layered public realm, planted interiors and a collection of residential-scale spaces to create a hotel that feels connected rather than compartmentalised.

TYPE
Business Hotel
APPROACH
Hospitality / Mixed Use
DESIGN
JVA

ARRIVAL / COMMON GROUND

Landscape is brought inside.

Curved built-in seating and generous planting soften the threshold between the exterior landscape and the interior public realm. The lobby works as more than a circulation space: it becomes a place to pause, meet and work.

II

Soft geometry is used throughout the public interiors to create a slower, more human rhythm. Seating, planting and circulation are arranged as one continuous landscape.

FORM / EDGE / SKYLINE

A layered skyline for a layered programme.

The massing combines taller hotel volumes with lower shared spaces. The result is a composition that keeps the public functions visually connected to the city while giving the upper levels a quieter presence.

SITE / ORGANISATION

One public ground, many ways to inhabit it.
