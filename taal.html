TAAL

INDIAN MUSIC EXPERIENCE STORE
THE PROJECT

A spatial experience shaped by rhythm, material and Indian musical culture.

TAAL is an Indian music experience store conceived as an immersive environment where architecture, instruments, craft and material come together. Warm earth tones, deep indigo surfaces, timber, cane and brass create a tactile interior rooted in Indian musical traditions.

The space uses arched niches, layered displays and a sculptural central island to turn the act of browsing into an experience. Instruments become part of the architecture, while the ceiling introduces a continuous rhythmic gesture above the store.
